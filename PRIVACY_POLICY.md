## Information We Do NOT Collect

**LearnSloka is designed with privacy in mind. We do NOT collect any of the following:**

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- User accounts or registration data
- Location data or GPS information
- Device contacts or calendar information
- Photos, videos, or media files from your device
- Biometric data
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Advertising identifiers
- Social media information
- Payment or financial information
- Any data that could identify you personally

## Information Stored Locally on Your Device

LearnSloka stores certain information locally on your device to enhance your learning experience. This data **never leaves your device** and includes:

### 1. Learning Progress Data
- **Last Position:** The timestamp and segment where you last paused in each stotra
- **Playback Speed:** Your preferred audio playback speed (0.5x to 2x)
- **Font Size:** Your preferred text size for comfortable reading
- **Theme Preference:** Your choice between light and dark mode

### 2. App Settings
- User interface preferences
- Audio player configurations
- Display settings

**Storage Method:** All data is stored using your device's local storage (HTML5 localStorage on web, native storage on Android). This data:
- Remains on your device only
- Is never transmitted to our servers
- Can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing browser data
- Is not backed up to any cloud service

## Audio and Content Files

### Content Sources
- All stotra audio files are included within the app installation

### Offline Functionality
- The app is designed to work completely offline after installation
